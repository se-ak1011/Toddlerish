Email drainedstore@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. A few common questions are answered below in case they save you a wait.

I bought "Unlock everything" but it's still locked. What do I do?
Open any locked behaviour or trick to reach the paywall, then tap Restore purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID for a completed purchase and unlocks the app without charging you again. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you purchased with.
Do I need an account?
No. Toddlerish has no accounts, no sign-in, and no login of any kind — everything works straight away.
Is my data private?
Yes — everything you save in the app stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
Can you add a behaviour or trick I'm dealing with?
We'd love to hear it — email us the behaviour and we'll consider it for a future update.
The app won't open, or something looks broken.
Try restarting the app, then your device, then reinstalling if that doesn't help. If it's still not working, email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll help you sort it out.
