Toddlerish

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Toddlerish ("the app", "we", "us") is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app touches, what it doesn't, and why.

The short version

Toddlerish doesn't have accounts, doesn't run ads, doesn't use analytics or tracking software, and doesn't collect or sell your personal information. Almost everything you do in the app — saving a favourite, telling it your child's age range — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The one exception is processing the optional one-time "Unlock everything" purchase, described below.

What's stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, using standard on-device storage. None of it is transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Behaviours and tricks you've saved as favourites
  • Your child's age range, if you choose to enter it during onboarding — entirely optional and skippable
  • Whether you've completed onboarding

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. There is no server copy, because there is no server.

In-app purchases

The optional "Unlock everything" purchase is a one-time payment processed by Apple's App Store and, on our side, by RevenueCat, a third-party service we use to verify purchases and keep unlocked content unlocked if you reinstall the app or switch devices. To do this, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier for your installation and your purchase/transaction details — it does not receive your name, contact details, or any other information you haven't already given Apple through the App Store itself.

Apple's own handling of your payment is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat's by its own Privacy Policy. We don't see or store your payment details ourselves at any point.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No account creation, so nothing is tied to your identity
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data to third parties beyond the purchase processing described above

Children's privacy

Toddlerish is designed to be used by parents and caregivers about their toddlers — it is not directed at, marketed to, or intended to be used directly by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional "child's age range" setting is a general range only, is not a name or identifier, is stored solely on the parent's own device, and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Your choices

Because everything is local, you're always in control: clear the app's data or uninstall it at any time to remove everything it stores. If you have a question about data RevenueCat holds related to a purchase, contact us below and we'll help point you in the right direction.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
